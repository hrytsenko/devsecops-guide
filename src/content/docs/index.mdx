## Model

Teams consist of people and agents, who work under the same controls and make and review changes interchangeably, unless a control requires a person.
Teams deploy continuously: once a change merges, only automated checks stand between it and production.

Each team owns its components and their changes, and delivers them through pipelines, which deploy them to environments.
The platform team owns the platform, which provides pipelines, environments, and backing services to every team.
It runs pipelines and environments on Kubernetes clusters that all teams share.
